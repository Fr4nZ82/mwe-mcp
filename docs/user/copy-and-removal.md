# A copy of your memory, or its removal

Both are real, both are complete, and both go through your admin — the buttons
are on your account's page in their console. Ask them.

**Take the copy first.** Once you are forgotten it cannot be built.

## A copy of everything

You get an archive holding: your wiki, the facts other people's wikis hold
**about** you with who said each one and when, the files you uploaded, and your
identity card.

## Removal

Your own memory goes and no copy is kept. Concretely:

- Your wiki and everything under it, including the wiki a consumer keeps for
  you, is erased where it stands — it does not go to the trash, and the window
  a deleted wiki normally gets does not apply.
- Every fact you said about yourself goes, and so does every behaviour rule
  about you: a rule handed to somebody else would become an instruction *about*
  you.
- The files you uploaded go, unless somebody else uploaded the same file.
- Your sign-in and aliases, your group memberships, the permission letting a
  consumer speak as you, the notices waiting for you and the recent
  conversation window all go.
- Your user id is spent. Nobody gets it afterwards — including you, if you come
  back: that would hand them everything the memory still keeps under the name.

## What stays, and why

**What other people said about you stays with them.** *"bob did a great job on
the client presentation"* is that colleague's memory of their own working life,
and it does not disappear because bob leaves. Your name stays written on such a
fact as a plain name — one the memory holds without it being anybody's account,
so it gives nobody the right to read or change anything of yours.

**What you said about other people stays where it is**, and only your name as
the one who said it is replaced, by an identity nobody can hold.

The record of what was done on the deployment stays, with the name anonymised
the same way.

## The one thing removal cannot reach

A backup taken before the day you are forgotten still holds everything, and
restoring one would bring you back whole. What happens to the backups already
on disk is your admin's decision, and the confirmation page they see says so.
