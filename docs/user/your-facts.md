# Your facts

**Nav: Facts.** `/dashboard/facts`

You arrive filtered on yourself: *the facts this memory holds about you —
wherever they are filed, including on other people's and groups' pages*.
**Everything I can read →** widens it to every fact your wikis grant you, other
people's included.

Two filters answer two different questions, and the page says so: **About**
narrows to what the memory holds about one person or group; **Wiki** narrows to
what is filed in one place. Alongside them are **Kind**, **Topic** (one word),
a **Recorded after** / **Recorded before** window, **Rows per page**, and a
checkbox to **include facts that no longer hold (replaced or forgotten)** — the
history is not deleted, it is closed, so this is how you read it.

The arrowed column headers sort. Clicking an id copies it.

## What each row tells you

Every fact carries, at least: **Status**, the **Wiki** it is filed in,
**About** (who or what it is about), **Said by**, **Also readable by**,
**Topics**, **What it says**, the dates it **Holds from** and **Holds until**,
when it was **Recorded**, **Last changed** and **Last recalled**, and how many
times it was recalled in the last 30 days. When a fact has been closed or
replaced, the last columns say when, by what, and why.

The **Actions** column carries **wiki** (open the page it sits on), **edit**,
and **delete** where you are allowed to. **edit** and **delete** appear on a
fact that is active and already filed: a capture the memory has not placed on a
page yet, and a fact that has already been replaced or forgotten, carry the
**wiki** link alone.

## Correcting a fact

**edit** opens one fact with three separate things you can do to it. The page
shows its current state first, so you always see what you are changing.

**Who may read it.** *About* and *Also readable by*. Applied directly, and the
next page is a receipt saying what changed. It is final: to put it back, make
the opposite change. You may do this to a fact **about you**.

**The dates it holds between.** *Holds from* and *Holds until* — leave a field
blank to keep that bound unchanged. Same gate, same receipt. This is how you
close a fact that has stopped being true without deleting it.

**Its words, its topics, its kind.** These go through the chat instead: you
fill in what should change, the form composes the request, and the chat asks
you to confirm before it applies anything. That is deliberate — changing what a
fact *says* creates a new version and closes the old one, and that cascade is
worth confirming.

## Forgetting a fact

**delete** appears on a fact **you said**. It forgets it: it leaves recall
immediately and survives only as a record that something was forgotten, which
you can still see with *include facts that no longer hold*.

A fact **somebody else** said about you is not yours to delete outright. Ask in
the chat and it becomes a request that goes to a vote among the people who can
read it — see [The chat](the-chat.md).

## Sections

The **Sections** tab beside Facts is a different thing and says so: what the
engine indexed out of the documentation wikis a coding assistant keeps. Each
page is cut into sections at its headings, and each section is what a
consumer's own deep search can match. They are **not** facts — no subject, no validity, no history,
because they are re-derived from the page every time it changes. Read-only: to
change what is indexed, edit the page.
