# Reminders and notices

Some things reach you without your asking. Four of them arrive **through your
assistant**, because that is where you are; the fifth waits for you in the
dashboard.

**A reminder.** When something you told the memory comes due — an appointment,
a commitment with a date on it — your assistant tells you. It is the only alarm
the engine rings, and it rings only for something already stored. A plan that
was closed by a newer plan is not a commitment, and does not ring.

**A fact somebody else minted for you.** If a fact *about you* was captured out
of somebody else's conversation, you are told, rather than discovering it on
your next recall. One notice per conversation, however many facts it produced,
and it carries the facts themselves and a link into this dashboard.

**A change made to a fact of yours.** When somebody's turn closed, re-filed or
changed the visibility of a fact that belongs to you, that is a notice too,
with a link to read what happened. The night's own housekeeping is silent: the
memory reorganises itself constantly, and reporting each split and merge would
be a diary nobody asked for.

**A question, when your card already says something else.** Your identity card
holds a handful of things there can only be one of: when you were born, where
you live, how to reach you. If a conversation states a *different* value for
one of them, nothing is written: your assistant asks which of the two is right,
quoting the one already on record and saying who said it and when. Answer, and
the memory does exactly what you said — keeps the old one, or replaces it. Say
nothing, and the card stays as it was. This is the one case where the memory
stops and asks before storing, and it is deliberate: two birth dates on one
card are worse than a question.

**Something waiting on your answer** waits here rather than reaching you: a
request to forget a fact you can read, and a disagreement about one of those
card values that the person who raised it was not entitled to settle. Both
light the badge in the top bar of the dashboard, and clicking it opens the chat
on exactly those items. See [The chat](the-chat.md).

If your assistant is not delivering these, that is a question for whoever runs
the server: the notices are queued for the consumer to collect, and a consumer
that never collects them never speaks.
