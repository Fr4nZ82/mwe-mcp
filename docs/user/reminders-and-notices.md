# Reminders and notices

Some things reach you without your asking. Three of them arrive **through your
assistant**, because that is where you are; the fourth waits for you in the
dashboard.

**A reminder.** When something you told the memory comes due — an appointment,
a commitment with a date on it — your assistant tells you. It is the only alarm
the engine rings, and it rings only for something already stored. A plan that
was closed by a newer plan is not a commitment, and does not ring.

**A fact somebody else minted for you.** If a fact *about you* was captured out
of somebody else's conversation, you are told, rather than discovering it on
your next recall. One notice per conversation, however many facts it produced,
and it carries the facts themselves and a link into this dashboard.

**A change made to a fact of yours.** When somebody's turn closed, re-filed or
changed the visibility of a fact that belongs to you, that is a notice too,
with a link to read what happened. The night's own housekeeping is silent: the
memory reorganises itself constantly, and reporting each split and merge would
be a diary nobody asked for.

**Something waiting on your answer** — a request to forget a fact you can read
— is the one that waits here: it lights the badge in the top bar of the
dashboard, and clicking it opens the chat on exactly those items. See
[The chat](the-chat.md).

If your assistant is not delivering these, that is a question for whoever runs
the server: the notices are queued for the consumer to collect, and a consumer
that never collects them never speaks.
