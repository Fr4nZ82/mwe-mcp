# Your first sign-in

## The invitation link

Your admin creates your account and hands you a link that looks like
`/dashboard/accept-invite/…`. It is **single-use** and it expires — 24 hours,
in a standard deployment. Open it and you get a page addressed to you: *"You
were invited to mwe-mcp as bob. Choose your password below — the admin will
never see it."*

Type a password (minimum 12 characters), confirm it, and press **Set password
and sign in**. If the link has already lapsed, ask for another; your admin can
mint one from your row on their Users page.

## The three-step welcome

You land on **Welcome**, which offers to give your memory a starting point in
three steps — the page counts them as *Step 1 of 3*. It is all optional:
anything you leave blank does not go into the memory, **Skip all** is a real
answer, and you can come back to it later.

**1 · Who you are.** Your name, nickname, a free introduction, date of birth,
address, pronouns, phone, work, and two settings worth getting right — the
first of them marked *Required* on the page:

- **Primary language.** This is the language your memory is *written in*, not
  only the language your assistant answers you in: every page the engine
  compiles for you is written in it. It arrives pre-filled from your browser —
  change it if it is wrong.
- **Time zone.** Times you speak — *"tomorrow at 9"* — are read in it.

Your **name** and your **nickname** do one more thing besides going into your
memory: they become the names the memory recognises you by, beside your user
id. From then on, when somebody else mentions you by either of them, what they
say is filed as being about you — and a name nobody has given it, however close
to yours, belongs to somebody else. Leave both blank and only your user id
reaches you, until your admin adds a name on your behalf.

There is also **Health & safety (always relevant)**: things an assistant should
keep in mind in every exchange, whatever the topic.

⚠️ Everything in step 1, health and safety included, is **public in your
wiki**: other people signed into this dashboard can read it. Write only what
you are happy to share, and leave blank anything you would rather keep private —
say that in ordinary conversation with your assistant instead, which keeps
facts private by default.

**2 · Your rules.** How the memory should treat your data. One choice —
private by default, shared with the relevant group when it makes sense, or
always private — plus who it should never be shared with, topics to always keep
private, and things to never store. Written once, they always apply.

**3 · The rest.** A few preferences and interests: favourite colour,
hobbies, food. Nothing binding; the memory files them wherever they fit.

**Save and go** finishes. What you wrote lands on your identity card — the one
page the memory serves whole, every time somebody talks to you.

## Then

You arrive on [your home page](your-home.md). If you want a second layer on
your sign-in, [your account page](your-account.md) has it.
