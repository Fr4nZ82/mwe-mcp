# Engineering log

Decision-log entries start from the 1.0 public release. The pre-1.0 engineering
journal (which tracked the private dogfood deployment day by day) lives in the
maintainer's private archive and is not part of the public repository.

Format of an entry: `- **YYYY-MM-DD** · area — **headline.** Body with links to
the design notes and planning pages it touches.`
